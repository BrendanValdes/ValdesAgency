## §4 — CPL FRAMEWORK (LOCKED — DO NOT CHANGE)

These three bands are the production truth for SonoView. They are also the baseline used across every other niche in the agency. **Do not change these without Julie's explicit approval via Brendan.**

| Band | CPL range | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 GOOD | **$20 – $35** | Profitable, healthy | Let it run. Don't touch. |
| 🟡 ACCEPTABLE | **$35 – $55** | Marginal | Watch. Optimize ad copy first. Don't pause. |
| 🔴 FIX | **$55+** | Bleeding money | Pause worst performer. Fix landing page first (NOT ad copy). |

### The math (why these bands exist)
- $150 blended AOV
- ~30% close rate from lead → booked session (estimate, validate with GHL data)
- Breakeven CPL = AOV × close rate = $150 × 0.30 = **$45**
- $20–35 = comfortable margin
- $35–55 = thin or losing on lead-level (LTV makes it OK if repeat + referrals factored)
- $55+ = losing money even with optimistic LTV assumptions

### How to react when CPL crosses bands
- 🟢 → 🟡: do nothing for 7 days. CPL fluctuates. Don't panic-edit.
- 🟡 → 🔴: trigger §10 performance reviewer. Diagnose, don't pause yet.
- 🔴 → still 🔴 after 14 days of optimization: route to Julie for pause decision.

## §9 — UPSELL + POST-VISIT SEQUENCE

### In-session upsell pattern (proven — drives the AOV bump)
The blended AOV is $150 even though entry is $129. The gap is filled by in-session upsells. Pattern:

1. Customer books entry-tier package online ($129)
2. In-session, technician offers add-ons:
   - Extra prints / framed photo
   - Heartbeat audio recording on USB / keychain
   - Extended session time
   - Video clip in addition to images
3. ~50% of entry-tier bookings upgrade to a higher AOV during the session
4. Result: blended AOV settles at ~$150

**Why this works:** the customer is already emotionally bought-in (they're literally seeing their baby on screen). Add-ons feel like extending the moment, not selling.

### Post-visit sequence (GHL-automated — see §7)

| Time after visit | Channel | Purpose | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 hours | SMS | Thank you + review request (link to GBP) | Review submitted |
| 48 hours | SMS | Soft nudge if no review yet | One nudge only — stop after this |
| 1 week | Email | Photo download reminder + cross-sell mid-pregnancy if customer is < 28 weeks | Click |
| 1 month | Email | Gender reveal package or 28-week milestone offer | Booking or unsubscribe |
| 3 months | Email | Newborn referral — recommend a local newborn photographer or birth photographer (referral partnership opportunity) | Click |

### Repeat booking nurture
Many customers come back for: **gender reveal → 28-week milestone → newborn shoot referral**. The post-visit sequence is engineered to surface the next milestone before the customer thinks about it.

## §10 — PERFORMANCE REVIEWER (DECISION TREES)

Mirror of `skills/ads.md` §7. Every diagnostic returns a specific fix, not "investigate further."

### §10.1 — CPL > $55 (🔴 FIX band)
**Diagnose in this order. Don't skip steps.**
1. Is conversion tracking still firing? Test a form submission yourself. If broken, that's the entire problem — fix tracking before anything else.
2. Are keyword and search terms still relevant? Pull last 14 days of search terms. Any spend on irrelevant terms (medical, diagnostic, free, DIY) → add as negatives.
3. Has CTR dropped vs 30-day baseline by >15%? If yes → headline issue → §10.3.
4. Has landing page conversion rate dropped below 4%? If yes → page issue → fix page first (§8), don't touch ads.
5. Has Quality Score on top keywords dropped below 6? If yes → page relevance issue → §10.4.
6. None of the above → check for new competitor in auction (auction insights report). If new entrant, slight CPC pressure is normal — give it 14 days before reacting.

### §10.2 — 50+ clicks, zero conversions
**This is a landing page problem, not an ad problem.** Diagnose:
1. Run §8 landing page checklist top to bottom. What's broken?
2. Test the form yourself end-to-end (submit it, check GHL, check Google Ads conversion fires)
3. Phone number on the page — is it clickable? Does it ring?
4. Page load speed — under 2.5s mobile?
5. Is the hero clinical/medical-looking? (If yes, brand drift — see §2)

**Action:** fix page. Pause the worst-performing ad group temporarily so you don't keep spending while diagnosing. Don't add more keywords or "fix the ad" — the ad isn't the problem.

### §10.3 — CTR < 2% with 200+ impressions
**Headline issue.**
1. Pull current RSA assets — which headlines are pulling weight?
2. Add 3–5 new headline variations (don't replace existing — add)
3. Test for 14 days
4. Prune the worst performer

**Headline patterns that have worked:** emotional ("See your baby smile"), local ("Reno's premium 3D/4D"), price transparency ("From $129"), gender reveal angle.

### §10.4 — Quality Score < 6
**Landing page relevance issue.**
1. Page H1 must include primary keyword + city ("3D/4D Ultrasound in Reno")
2. Above-fold copy must match the ad's promise exactly
3. Page load under 2.5s mobile
4. LocalBusiness schema implemented (see `skills/website.md`)
5. Mobile-first design, 44px tap targets

### §10.5 — Conversion rate drops suddenly
**Recent change audit. Something changed — find it.**
1. What changed in Google Ads in the last 7 days? (check change history)
2. What changed in GHL workflows? Any new automation? Any disabled?
3. What changed on the landing page? (check Wix edit history)
4. What changed in conversion tracking? Test a fresh submission end-to-end.
5. What changed seasonally? (Reno tourism patterns, holidays, school calendar)

**Rule:** if you can't find a recent change, don't make a new one. Wait 7 days. Conversion rate fluctuates; sometimes it just dips.

### §10.6 — The "$5 cap removed" failure mode (don't repeat)
**Documented for future Brendan / agents:** removing the $5 CPC cap once doubled CPL within 48 hours. The cap is the safety mechanism — it forces Google to find the cheapest profitable inventory rather than auction-warring against bigger advertisers. **Never remove without 30 days of GOOD-band CPL data + Julie's signoff + a 7-day rollback plan.**

### §10.7 — The "bid strategy switched mid-week" failure mode
**Documented:** switched bid strategies on SonoView mid-week once. Reset learning phase. $200 burn before the new strategy stabilized. **Never change bid strategy outside of the §5.6 reactivation protocol Day 14+ window.**
