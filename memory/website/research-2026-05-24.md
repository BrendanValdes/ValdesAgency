## 1. Reference site teardown (Firecrawl branding extraction)

Six sites targeted. Two blocked Firecrawl on browser check (Active Theory + Igloo Inc — both require WebGL2 to render at all, which is a brutal user experience filter and a signal in itself). Four returned full branding extractions.

### Cuberto (cuberto.com)
- **Scheme:** Light (white bg, black text)
- **Type:** Suisse Intl (Klim, premium Swiss sans). 90px h1, 24px body.
- **Accent:** Red (#FC0019) — surgical, sparse use
- **Buttons:** Pure black, 1000px radius (full pill)
- **Grid:** 4px base unit, 20px corner radius on cards
- **Takeaway:** Big editorial type + restrained palette + pill buttons. Not our scheme but the typographic confidence is the bar.

### Lusion (lusion.co)
- **Scheme:** Light primary, dark hero
- **Type:** Aeonik (CoType, very premium)
- **Accent:** Cyan (#00FFFF) for links, electric energy
- **Buttons:** White-fill pill (102px radius), dark soft shadow
- **Takeaway:** 3D-heavy. Their content is video reel + immersive cases. Our case-study substitute is "How It Works" methodology depth.

### Locomotive (locomotive.ca)
- **Scheme:** DARK (#000 bg) — closest match to our direction
- **Type:** LocomotiveNew (custom) + HelveticaNowDisplay. 79px h1.
- **Accent:** Red + blue (#312DFB) — multi-accent
- **Buttons:** ZERO border radius. Squared, brutalist. Strong stance.
- **Takeaway:** The proof that brutalist-modern works at premium ceiling. Their buttons being SQUARE (not pill like Cuberto/Lusion) is a real aesthetic position. Pitch: we go pill OR square consistently, not mixed.

### Active Theory + Igloo Inc — blocked Firecrawl on browser check
Both gate the entire site behind WebGL2 capability checks. Translation: they assume their audience has bleeding-edge browsers and devices, and they don't care about the rest. This IS the stance. Not appropriate for us — service business owners might browse on a 3-year-old Android.

**Implication:** Our caliber bar is "looks like AT/Lusion at first glance" without locking out users on older Safari or budget Android. WebGL fallbacks mandatory.

## 2. 2026 Awwwards trend pulse (Exa search)

Ten SOTDs and nominees pulled from the last 90 days. Five clean signal patterns:

1. **Pure 2-color palettes dominate.** Silent House (Apr 28): black + white. Aino.agency (May 20): #f5f5f0 + #181818. Studio Namma (May 8): #111 + #E4E4E4. The list goes on.

2. **The closest cousin to our direction is Darknode** (Mar 21 SOTD, military drone interceptor unit) — palette is **#F90000 red + #14181E near-black**. Two colors only. Wins on restraint.

3. **Type as hero, not images.** Aino's whole site is text-as-medium with ASCII + 2D physics in 30kb JS. Horeca Social's nominee was sticky-scroll typography that grows. VECT. is massive Japanese typography in motion. The trend is type-led, not image-led.

4. **Cinematic preloader → reveal**. Multiple SOTDs open with a branded preloader that resolves into a hero. Worth considering for our hero load.

5. **Solo-built, pure HTML/CSS/JS sites are winning.** VECT. shipped without a framework. Aino is 30kb. The Awwwards jury is rewarding craft and restraint over framework abundance. We're using Next.js + React because we have to (for components/scaffolding speed), but the design language we ship should LOOK like a hand-crafted single file.

**The big synthesis:** 2026's Awwwards bar is **bold restraint**. Less color, more confident type, fewer effects done extremely well. Cuberto's old multi-color playful aesthetic is dated. Darknode and Aino are where the puck is going.

## 4. Color palette — PROPOSED REFINEMENT (needs decision)

### Originally locked (goal command)
- Black: #0A0A0A or #000000
- White: #FFFFFF
- Orange: warm premium #FF6B35 to #E85D04
- Red: deep oxblood #8B0000 to #B91C1C

### Phase 0 finding
2026 Awwwards SOTDs rarely ship 4 brand colors. They ship 2 brand colors with maybe one moment accent. Going to market with 4 visible brand colors reads as a 2022 design language, not 2026.

### Phase 0 recommendation
**Ship as a 2-color brand to the eye, with red as a "power moment" reserved for 1-2 deliberate uses.**

```
FOUNDATION (always present)
  --ink: #0A0A0A         (true black foundation, slightly warm)
  --paper: #F4F1EC       (off-white with warmth, NOT pure white — matches Aino direction)

BRAND ACCENT (the orange — owns the brand)
  --signal: #E85D04      (warm premium orange, deep saturation)
  --signal-bleed: #FF6B35 (lighter mix for gradients only — never solid fills)

POWER ACCENT (red — restricted)
  --power: #B91C1C       (deep oxblood, used ONLY for):
    1. Hover state on primary CTA
    2. Active state in the AI Team service block (the "agents activating" moment)
    Two uses, total. Anywhere else, the red is the orange.

NEUTRALS (text-only support)
  --ink-soft: #1A1A1A    (panels, surfaces inside dark sections)
  --paper-dim: #C8C4BD   (de-emphasized text on dark)
```

Rationale: This is essentially Darknode's playbook (black + bold accent, sparing red as power) but with our orange owning the brand. Keeps the Awwwards 2-color discipline while still honoring "high-end tech with a confident accent."

**G1 decision needed:** approve this refinement OR ship the original 4-color palette as locked.
