## Executive Summary

Full Pool Services is, on the fundamentals, one of the strongest-positioned shops in the valley that doesn't know it: it's the most complete price-publisher of any competitor (7 priced services, where six of nine rivals hide pricing entirely), it's competitively priced at/below the ~$150 market median, it already ranks organic first-page in Summerlin and Las Vegas, and it already replies to its reviews. Its problem is conversion, not visibility or price — anchored by a **3.9★ rating on just 21 reviews, the only sub-4.6 score in a field running 4.6 to 5.0**, and made worse because its recurring complaints (surprise price hikes, skipped visits, weak communication) directly undercut the transparency it should be famous for. The path is tight and mostly cheap: an automated review engine plus a visit-notification/locked-pricing SOP to fix the rating at its root, online booking layered onto the prices it already lists, trust badges lifted out of the footer, and only then trust-led ads into the two metros where it already shows up. Fix the proof and the presentation and FPS out-converts rivals it's currently losing to on a star rating alone.
