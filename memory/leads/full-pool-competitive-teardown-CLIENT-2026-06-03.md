## G. Opportunities for Full Pool Services (prioritized)

1. **Grow the review rating and volume.** At 3.9★ on 21 reviews against competitors at 4.6–5.0, a consistent system for inviting satisfied customers to review after each service is the single highest-impact move, since the star rating is the first thing most prospective customers compare.
2. **Tighten scheduling communication.** Because the most common review themes relate to visit timing, price changes, and communication, a simple "heads-up before each visit and confirmed pricing" practice would address those themes directly — and reinforce the transparent pricing the company is already strong on.
3. **Add online booking and instant quotes.** With eight of nine competitors offering no online booking, and Full Pool Services already publishing its prices, adding a book-online option would create a clear convenience advantage and capture customers who decide outside business hours.
4. **Feature certifications prominently and own the transparency message.** The NSPF, CPO, Health-District, and BBB credentials currently sit in the website footer; moving them up top, paired with a clear "published, no-surprise pricing" message, would highlight two strengths few competitors can match.
5. **Targeted local advertising in Summerlin and Henderson.** With organic visibility already in place and the items above addressed, focused local advertising built around transparent pricing and credentials would reach high-intent customers efficiently.

## Executive Summary

Full Pool Services is stronger on fundamentals than its online presence suggests. It is the most complete price-publisher of any company reviewed — listing seven services with real prices while six of nine competitors publish none — it is competitively priced at or below the roughly $150 market median, it already appears on the first page of organic search in Summerlin and Las Vegas, and it already replies to customer reviews. The clearest growth opportunities are in reputation and online conversion rather than service quality or visibility: the public rating (3.9★ on 21 reviews) is the only score below 4.6 in a field running 4.6 to 5.0, and the most common review themes — scheduling, communication, and unexpected price changes — point to process rather than craftsmanship. Strengthening the review rating, tightening visit communication, adding online booking onto the prices already published, and bringing existing certifications to the top of the site would let Full Pool Services' genuine strengths show up where customers actually make their decision.
