## V2 SCRIPT

OPENER:
"Hey, this [Name]? ...Cool. Brendan here, calling out of Vegas. I know this is random. I'm not pitching you anything. Just had one quick question for whoever runs lead follow up on your end. 30 seconds and I'm out of your hair. Fair?"

IF NOT THE RIGHT PERSON:
"Got it. They around or is there a better time to catch them?"

(if not available) "What's their name so I know who to ask for?"

(get the name, hang up, call back)

IF THAT'S THEM:
"Perfect, [Name]. I'm guessing most of your new business comes from word of mouth and repeat customers. Is that fair?"

(let them answer fully. don't interrupt)

IF WORD OF MOUTH / REFERRALS:
"Yeah, that's the dream when it's working. Does it ever just dry up on you?"

(let them answer)

IF IT DRIES UP:
"That's gotta be stressful when it hits. What does that look like, jobs just slow down or does it actually hurt the business?"

(let them go deep. this is where they sell themselves)

IF RELIABLE:
"Good for you. Out of curiosity though, do leads ever come in and just not get responded to fast enough, where you lose them before you even know it?"

(most will admit it happens)

IF THEY DO STUFF ONLINE:
"Nice. Is that actually bringing in new jobs consistently or is it more hit or miss?"

(they'll almost always admit hit or miss)

"And when a lead does come in, how fast are you guys usually getting back to them?"

(this surfaces the follow up gap without you pointing it out)

BRIDGE:
"Yeah. Talked to a guy in Henderson last week, same exact thing. Leads were coming in but by te time he called back the homeowner already booked someone else. He figured he was losing four jobs a month just to slow follow up. You ever feel like that's happening to you?"

(let them answer. if yes you're in)

THE MAGIC QUESTION:
"How many extra jobs a month would actually move the needle for you, where you stop even thinking about it?"

(let them say a numbehr. do not fill the silence. then respond:)

"[X] a month. Real quick, what's an average pool job worth to you, four hundred? Five? Okay, so call it [X] at four fifty. That's [X x 450] a month, round numbers that's [X x 450 x 12] a year you're not getting right now. That's not a nice bonus. That's a different size business."

THE SOFT ASK:
"Look, this might not even be a fit for you and that's fine. But would it be worth ten minutes later this week so I can show you what we've been doing for other pool guys in your spot? No pitch, just see if it even makes sense. Honest heads up, I'm only taking on two or three pool companies in Vegas this quarter because I want to actually deliver. Not a sales line, just where I'm at."

(wait. don't say another word)

ONCE THEY SAY YES:
"Perfect. How's [Day] at [time]?"

(fill day/time live: 2-3 biz days out from dial, 10am or 3pm slot. let them confirm or counter)

"Cool. I'll shoot you a text to lock it in. Best number to reach you?"

(hang up immediately. do not keep talking)

IF NOT INTERESTED:
"Totally fair. Real quick before I let you go, is business just good right now or has this kind of thing not worked for you in the past?"

(let them answer. don't defend yourself)

IF ALREADY HAVE SOMEONE:
"Got it. They handling all of it or just certain pieces?"

(silence. they'll name the gap themselves)

"And is that actually moving the needle for you or is it more just kind of there?"

IF HOW MUCH:
"Honestly depends on what you actually need. That's what the ten minutes is for. If it's not a fit I'll tell you straight up, not going to waste your time."

IF SEND ME INFO:
"Yeah I can do that. Honestly it won't mean much without context though. Ten minutes on a call and I can show you exactly what we'd do, way more useful than an email. [Day] at [time] work, or later in the week?"

IF WANT TO THINK ABOUT IT:
"Yeah, sounds like you've been burned before. Easiest thing is just lock in a time. If it doesn't make sense after we talk you tell me right there on the call. Fair?"

VOICEMAIL (18-22 seconds):
"Hey [Name], Brendan from Valdes Agency out of Vegas, 702-523-8826. Calling because I noticed something about how your pool business is showing up online that's probably costing you jobs every month. Worth a quick conversation, that's it. Call me back when you get a chance, 702-523-8826. Talk soon."

CALLBACK:
"Hey [Name], Brendan from Valdes Agency in Vegas. Left you a voicemail [last week / Tuesday] about something I noticed with your pool business. Got 30 seconds for the reason I called?"

IF NO:
"No problem. Short version, just had one question about how leads are getting handled on your end. 60 seconds, then you tell me if it's worth more?"
